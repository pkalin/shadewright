# The name

**Shadewright** is two old words put together.

**Shade** is shadow, the half-light where the dark elves of fantasy live and work: unseen, patient, very
good at their craft. The project's first brief asked for a name from that world, and the hooded figure in
the logo comes from it too. It fits what the operator does: it watches a cluster from the background,
reads every event and log line, and stays out of the way until it has something worth saying.

**Wright** is the Old English *wyrhta*, a maker or craftsman, the word that survives in *shipwright*,
*wheelwright* and *playwright*. A wright does not command things into being; they build them, and someone
else decides whether the work is good enough to use.

That is the whole design in one word. Shadewright does not cast fixes onto a cluster. It crafts them, as a
small, typed, reviewable change in Git, and hands the work over: your review rules decide whether it
merges, and your GitOps controller is the one that puts it in place.

## A wraith on the way

The first domain bought for the project was `shadewrith.io`, one letter short, which reads more like
*shade-wraith*. It was kept: it redirects here, a small ghost of the naming.
