# Design and decisions

The reasoning behind Shadewright, complete and in one place. It is for the reader who wants to know why it behaves as it does, not only how to run it.

- [Design](design.md): the whole system design in sixteen numbered sections, from goals and architecture to cost and prior art. Section 15 lists decisions made and open questions.
- [Threat model](threat-model.md): what is protected, the trust boundaries, and each guarantee with its code and tests, marked built or planned.
- [Architecture decisions](adr/index.md): the ADRs, one page per decision, with their status.
- [The name](name.md): where *Shadewright* comes from.
